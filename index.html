Why it works

Built on research, not bro-science.

Every rule in the guide has a reason under it. Here are the big ones, in plain words.

Hard sets grow you

Hypertrophy (muscle growth) increases as sets are taken closer to failure. So the guide makes every set count.

[1] Robinson et al., 2024

Short on time? Still works.

More weekly sets still tend to build more muscle. But when time is short, a handful of truly hard sets per muscle works well.

[2] Schoenfeld et al., 2017

Train the stretch

Training at long muscle lengths (the stretched part of the rep) likely produces slightly more growth. The exercises are picked with that in mind.

[3] Wolf et al., 2023

Protein, made simple

30-40 g of protein per meal is a practical minimum. There's no rigid ceiling.

[4] Trommelen et al., 2023

Lean is the base

Higher body fat is linked to lower testosterone. That's one more reason the plan gets you lean first.

[5] Corona et al., 2013

Sleep is part of training

One week of short sleep can lower testosterone by 10-15%. Recovery is in the plan, not an extra.

[6] Leproult & Van Cauter, 2011

A natural target

An FFMI (muscle for your height) of 22-23 at 10-12% body fat is an athletic, natural target. Natural lifters rarely go past about 25. That's the look this plan aims for. No TRT needed.

[7] Kouri et al., 1995

Shoulders make the shape

Wide shoulders and a narrow waist are what read as "built" in a shirt. The plan trains side delts often for that reason.
